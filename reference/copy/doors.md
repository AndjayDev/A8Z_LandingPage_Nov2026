# Matching customers for your business

Somewhere this week, a company is looking for exactly what you sell. The need is real, and often the money is already set aside. It has simply not met you yet.

You know how hard that company is to find. You can buy a list and guess, or advertise and wait, and both cost money before a single buyer has said yes.

We work the other way round.

Every week we add to a network of companies that have shown, in one way or another, what they need right now. A brand with a launch coming and no film crew. A care home that cannot fill its shifts. A school that has to rebuild its network-infrastructure. A fast-scaling SaaS firm racing a compliance deadline. Next to them, we keep the businesses that can do that work well.

When you tell us what you sell and who you serve best, we look for your match in that pool. Often it is already there.

Then comes the careful part. We speak to the buyer first. We check that the need is real, that it is still open, and that you are the right fit for it. Only then do we introduce you.

So your first conversation is with a company that wants to have it.

It starts with one talk with us. Tell us what you sell, in your own words. We will tell you honestly what we see for you, and what we do not.

# Access deals and partnerships

Some needs cannot wait. A launch date is fixed. A shift has to be covered. A new market opens, and you need a partner who already knows the ground.

The right company for that exists. Finding it is the slow part. You search, you ask around, you sit through pitches from firms that were never a fit. And the date comes closer.

We keep that search running all year, so you never start from zero.

Every week we add to a network of businesses with something real to offer: suppliers with room to take on work, distributors looking for a product like yours, firms open to a partnership. Next to them we keep the companies that need them. When you tell us what you are looking for, we look in that network for the ones that fit. An introduction rarely takes long.

Not long ago, a nursing home short on nurses asked us one question: where is the agency based?

It was in the same city.

Before we introduce anyone, we check. Can they do the work? Can they start in time? Are they close enough? We get the answers in writing, and you see them first. We do not represent the other side. And if a company turns out to be materially different from what we described, we find you another.

One talk with us is enough to begin. Tell us what you need, and by when.
