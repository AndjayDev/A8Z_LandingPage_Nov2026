# **Connecting a Cleaning Company With a New Orleans Airbnb Owner**

**Industry:** Vacation Rental Cleaning  
**Customer:** Angie Nola Maids  
**Transaction:** $3,000

### **The Opportunity**

Angie Nola Maids, a cleaning company based in New Orleans, was looking to acquire additional Airbnb and vacation rental customers.

We identified an Airbnb owner managing multiple properties, including an eight-bedroom duplex, who needed additional cleaning capacity. Her existing cleaner was struggling to accommodate Sunday turnovers and same-day guest check-ins.

### **What We Delivered**

We contacted the property owner and confirmed that she needed additional cleaning services.

We then connected her directly with Angie Nola Maids, which already specialized in vacation rental cleaning and could accommodate recurring schedules and last-minute turnovers, depending on availability.

### **The Result**

Angie Nola Maids paid Acceler8zone $3,000 for the introduction and was pleased to be connected with an Airbnb owner who had an immediate need for its services and the potential to become a recurring customer.
