# **Connecting a Pharmaceutical Wholesaler With a Healthcare Network**

**Industry:** Pharmaceutical Wholesale  
**Customer:** Advanced Medical Sales  
**Opportunity:** Healthcare network operating 60+ clinics

### **The Opportunity**

Advanced Medical Sales, a pharmaceutical wholesaler, was interested in connecting with pharmacies and healthcare organizations actively purchasing medication.

We identified a Texas healthcare network operating more than 60 clinics and four pharmacies. Its existing supplier was unable to provide sufficient quantities of several medications, leaving the organization searching for an additional pharmaceutical wholesaler.

The network had an immediate purchasing requirement, including approximately 400 bottles of medication per month, and was prepared to speak with a qualified supplier within 24 hours.

### **What We Delivered**

We connected Advanced Medical Sales directly with the healthcare network's purchasing representative.

Before making the introduction, we confirmed that the wholesaler met the organization's distribution, accreditation and regulatory requirements.

We also obtained the wholesaler's product information, including medication availability and pricing, and supplied the relevant documentation to facilitate the commercial discussion.

### **The Result**

Advanced Medical Sales received a direct introduction to a healthcare network with an immediate, documented need for pharmaceutical supplies and the potential for repeat orders across its pharmacy operations.

The introduction was positively received, giving the wholesaler direct access to an interested purchasing representative.
