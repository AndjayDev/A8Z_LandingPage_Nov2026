# **Connecting a Florida Technology Company With a School District**

**Industry:** Education & Technology  
**Customer:** DGR Systems  
**Transaction:** $10,000

### **The Opportunity**

DGR Systems, a Florida technology company, was interested in securing additional school infrastructure projects.

We identified an active opportunity with the School District of Manatee County, which was purchasing 1,510 Wi-Fi 7 access points, network switches, and related equipment for its existing school facilities.

The district was looking for a qualified company capable of completing the project without disrupting classroom activities or testing schedules.

### **What We Delivered**

We established direct contact with the school district and confirmed that its purchasing process was still open.

After discussing the opportunity with DGR Systems, we provided the project requirements and equipment schedule and arranged a direct introduction to the district's representative.

### **The Result**

DGR Systems paid Acceler8zone $10,000 for the introduction and was pleased with the outcome, having been connected directly with a school district actively purchasing the infrastructure services it offered.
